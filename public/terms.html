HomeOps SMS Program — Terms & Conditions

Program name: HomeOps Household Assistant Messaging

Program description: HomeOps is a private, single-household family assistant. This messaging program sends household-assistant messages to members of the operating household only: replies to questions a member texts to the household's number, reminders about family calendar events and tasks, and one-time phone-verification codes.

Enrollment / consent

Only household members can enroll. A member adds their own mobile number inside the HomeOps app, receives a one-time verification code by text, and confirms it in the app. No number receives recurring messages without completing this verification and opt-in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with household activity — typically a few messages per week, plus direct replies whenever a member texts the household number.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. HomeOps itself does not charge recipients.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any message to cancel and stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. You can also remove or opt out your contact method inside the HomeOps app at any time.

Support

For support, contact the household administrator who operates this HomeOps deployment, or use the in-app Messages screen.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. Phone numbers are never sold or shared with third parties.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
