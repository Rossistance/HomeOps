## Intent (verbatim prompt)
"Every Friday at 4 PM, draft and send an outgoing email to the grandparents' email address detailing our weekly family highlights."

## Acceptance criteria (observable, testable — no bare "works/fast")
- AC1: The schedule trigger fires exactly once per week, at Friday 16:00 in the configured local timezone.
- AC2: outlook.send's `to` field equals the configured grandparents' email address exactly.
- AC3: The email body enumerates the family highlights collected for the current week window (Mon-Fri or configured range), with each highlight attributable to a source event/note.
- AC4: outlook.send is not called a second time for the same `weekOf` value (no duplicate weekly sends on retrigger/retry).
- AC5: If no highlights were collected for the week, the workflow either skips the send or sends an explicit "no highlights this week" body — never a silently empty body.
