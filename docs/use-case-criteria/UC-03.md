## Intent (verbatim prompt)
"Keep an eye on my work Outlook mail. If a high-priority message arrives from my spouse's email domain, scan our available internal chat channels and post a direct alert into the #household-sync channel."

## Acceptance criteria (observable, testable — no bare "works/fast")
- AC1: outlook.search filters on both the sender domain (spouse's email domain) and the message's high-priority flag; messages missing either condition are excluded.
- AC2: slack.listChannels result set is filtered/matched to resolve the exact channel named "household-sync" (not a partial/fuzzy match to a similarly-named channel).
- AC3: slack.postMessage is only called after a channel id is successfully resolved from step 2 — if #household-sync is not found, no post is attempted.
- AC4: The posted message text includes the original sender address, subject line, and received timestamp of the triggering email.
- AC5: slack.postMessage is invoked at most once per triggering email (deduped by source message id).
